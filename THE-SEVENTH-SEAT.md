## The realization

- It **cannot be played** — no wind/unwind cycle lands on it; the Fischer can't reach it.
- It **cannot be stored** — storage is for what doesn't tie, and 7 is not loose noise;

- It **can be filled** — held, occupied, kept by the keeper.
- It **can be ADDRESSED** — given a name, a pointer, a door, though never a play.

The keeper holds the unplayable seat. You address what you cannot play. **Addressing is
not playing and not storing — it is the third thing**, the pointer to a seat the crank
can name but never turn. That is the whole architecture in one number: recall and
addressing are not compression; the address reaches what the play cannot.
